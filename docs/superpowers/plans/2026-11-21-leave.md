# Leave (CafeOS Milestone 3) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Leave types, holidays, ledger balances with yearly and monthly grants, leave requests with HR decisions and safeguards, and the attendance and rota link, on the TEST database.

**Architecture:** This follows the Attendance module.
- **Tables and writes:** new tables with row-level security. The ledger and requests are written only through SECURITY DEFINER functions that take identity from the session; types and holidays are written directly under RLS. Grants, accrual and year-close run from the daily maintenance job.
- **Code:** a pure `rules.ts`, a service `leave.ts` that calls the functions, and Next.js 16 screens under `/leave`.

**Tech Stack:** PostgreSQL 17 (btree_gist installed), Prisma 7 with `@prisma/adapter-pg`, Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, vitest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-21-leave-design.md` (approved 21 Nov 2026, including the "Decisions added after the builder check").

## Global Constraints
- **Databases:** TEST database only. Migrations are applied by the owner's one-time approval (`prisma migrate` is denied to Claude). Never edit a migration once it is applied: add a new one. Dry-run every migration with `scratchpad/dry-attendance.cjs` first.
- **Commits:** nothing is committed, pushed or released without the owner's OK. The checkbox "Commit" steps below mean "stage for the milestone commit"; one commit at the end, with approval.
- **Roles:** exactly three roles. Permissions are checked by key, never by role name.
- **New permission** `leave:manage` ("Set up leave types, holidays and balance adjustments") for SUPER_ADMIN and HR_MANAGER. Existing keys: `leave:read`, `leave:approve`, `self:leave_apply`, `self:leave_read`.
- **Identity:** organisation and person come from the session (`app_org_id()`, `app_user_id()`, `app_my_employee_id()`), never from a form. The only exception is the employee id in HR-on-behalf entry, which is checked against the organisation.
- **Rights:** every new table starts with `REVOKE ALL ... FROM hr_runtime`, then exact grants. Ledger and requests: SELECT only.
- **Function conventions:**
  - SECURITY DEFINER functions: `SET search_path = pg_catalog, public, pg_temp`, `REVOKE ALL FROM PUBLIC`, and grants only on the entry points.
  - Lock order: employee row FOR SHARE, then `pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtextextended('leave:'||org||':'||employee,0))`, then rows.
  - Any id that is missing or belongs to another organisation gives the same `not_found`.
- **Dates:** local DATE values in the organisation's time zone (`att_org_timezone`). The leave year is the calendar year. A request never crosses 31 Dec / 1 Jan.
- **Day values:** NUMERIC(5,1), in steps of 0.5.
- **Seed values:** CL, SL, EL (MONTHLY), CO (NONE), UL (unpaid), all seeded with 0 days per year and 0 carry-over, `configured = false`. No statutory numbers anywhere.
- **Limits:** back-dated requests up to 31 days; future requests up to 365 days.
- **Year close:** runs on 1 Feb (carry-over and expiry of the previous year). New-year grants are written on 1 Jan.
- **Accrual month:** a month counts if the person is employed on its 15th.

## Owner's added tests (21 Nov 2026)
Each is pinned to a task below:
1. A same-day half-day request counts 0.5, not 1.0. **Task 2**
2. Approval with existing attendance, or with the employee already clocked in, never double-counts the day. **Task 3**
3. If the day count changes before approval (a holiday added, the pattern changed), HR sees the updated count and balance impact before deciding. **Task 2** adds `app_leave_preview`; **Task 4** shows it.
4. Changing or deactivating a leave type does not change historical ledger balances. **Task 2**
5. The "who is off" calendar follows the visibility rules: names and type only, never notes; organisation only; HR only. **Task 4**
6. The `app_clock_in` changes keep the approved location behaviour: clock-in from anywhere (no network or GPS enforcement), with the outlet taken from the matched shift, otherwise the home outlet, and the device details recorded. **Task 3**

## Review Focus
1. A request whose days all fall on weekly offs or holidays (count 0) must be refused, not stored as a 0-day leave. Tested in Task 2.
2. Two simultaneous requests or approvals for the same person must not overspend the balance. Tested in Task 2 with a two-connection race.
3. Cancelling approved leave that is partly in the past: only the future full days are RESTORED, and the past days stay TAKEN. Tested in Task 2.
4. Leave at a closed or other-organisation outlet, or a holiday added after approval: the stored count must not change. Tested in Task 2.
5. Approving leave over a shift the person is already clocked into today: the open record is untouched, and the shift for today is not cancelled if it has started. Tested in Task 3.

### Task 2: Day count, requests, decisions, ledger functions

**Files:**
- Create: `prisma/migrations/20261123000100_leave_functions/migration.sql`
- Test: `tests/db/leave.test.ts` (add describes)

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: the Task 1 tables, plus `att_org_timezone(uuid)`, `app_has_permission(text)`, `app_my_employee_id()`, `att_require_hr()`-style helpers, and `employee_shift_patterns.work_days`.
- **Produces SQL:**
  - **Internal (not granted to `hr_runtime`):**
    - `leave_count_days(p_org uuid, p_employee uuid, p_start date, p_end date, p_start_half bool, p_end_half bool) RETURNS numeric`.
    - `leave_available(p_org uuid, p_employee uuid, p_type uuid, p_year int) RETURNS numeric`: the ledger sum minus PENDING days.
    - `leave_on_full_leave(p_employee uuid, p_day date) RETURNS bool`.
  - **Entry points (granted to `hr_runtime`):**
    - `app_request_leave(p_type uuid, p_start date, p_end date, p_start_half bool, p_end_half bool, p_note text) RETURNS uuid`.
    - `app_withdraw_leave(p_request uuid) RETURNS void`.
    - `app_decide_leave(p_request uuid, p_approve bool, p_note text) RETURNS void`. The note is required to reject. On approval it recounts the days, writes TAKEN, and calls `leave_cancel_shifts` (Task 3).
    - `app_hr_record_leave(p_employee uuid, p_type uuid, p_start date, p_end date, p_start_half bool, p_end_half bool, p_note text) RETURNS uuid`: APPROVED directly.
    - `app_cancel_leave(p_request uuid, p_reason text) RETURNS void`.
    - `app_adjust_leave(p_employee uuid, p_type uuid, p_year int, p_days numeric, p_reason text) RETURNS void`.
    - `app_leave_housekeeping(p_today date DEFAULT NULL) RETURNS int`: maintenance mode only; `p_today` may only move the date back, for tests.
    - `app_leave_preview(p_request uuid) RETURNS TABLE(stored_days numeric, current_days numeric, available numeric, available_after numeric)`: HR only (`leave:read`), read-only. It recounts with today's holidays and pattern, giving the balance impact before a decision.
- **Error codes:** `leave_zero_days`, `leave_crosses_year`, `leave_too_far_back`, `leave_too_far_ahead`, `leave_insufficient_balance`, `leave_overlap`, `leave_note_required`, `leave_type_not_configured`, `leave_type_inactive`, `request_not_pending`, `own_request`, `leave_already_started`, `decision_note_required`, `reason_required`, `adjust_negative_balance`, `no_employee_record`, `employee_not_active`, `not_found`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests** (`describe("leave requests and balances")`):
  - `counts only working days`: with standard work days {1..6}, a holiday on Wednesday and a Mon–Sun request, expect 5. A half day on Monday's start gives 4.5. A request of Sunday only gives `leave_zero_days`.
  - `refuses paid leave beyond the available balance but allows unpaid`: with CL configured at 2, a 3-day CL request gives `leave_insufficient_balance`; a 3-day UL request succeeds. A pending 1.5-day CL request makes another 1-day CL request give `leave_insufficient_balance`.
  - `approval writes TAKEN once, rejection needs a note, nobody approves their own request unless sole approver`: the ledger for that request is exactly one TAKEN of −days. Rejecting without a note gives `decision_note_required`. HR deciding their own request gives `own_request` while the SA exists; once the SA is suspended, it succeeds with `self_decided = true`.
  - `HR records leave for staff without a login`: the employee has no member; status APPROVED, with `requested_by` = the HR user.
  - `cancelling restores only days not yet taken`: approved leave from yesterday to tomorrow (3 days), cancelled today by HR, writes RESTORED +2 (today and tomorrow). An employee cancelling after the start gives `leave_already_started`.
  - `grants, monthly accrual and year close are idempotent` (maintenance, with `p_today`):
    - YEARLY 12 days; a joiner on 10 Mar gets a GRANT of 10 (Mar–Dec);
    - MONTHLY 12 days gives ACCRUAL 1.0 per counting month, and running twice adds nothing;
    - on 1 Feb, carry-over 5 with a balance of 7 gives CARRY_OVER +5 into the new year and EXPIRED −7 in the old year.
  - `a holiday added after approval does not change the stored count`.
  - Owner 1, `a same-day half-day request counts 0.5`: start = end = Monday, start_half = true; days = 0.5 (not 1.0). Its ledger TAKEN is −0.5 after approval.
  - Owner 3, `HR sees the recount before deciding`: request Mon–Fri (5); HR adds a holiday on Wednesday; `app_leave_preview` returns stored 5, current 4, and available_after = available − 4; approval writes TAKEN −4 and stores days = 4.
  - Owner 4, `changing or deactivating a type keeps history`: after a GRANT of 12 and TAKEN −2, HR changes days_per_year to 20 and deactivates the type. The balance for that year is still 10, the ledger lines are unchanged, and new requests on the inactive type give `leave_type_inactive`.
  - `two simultaneous approvals cannot overspend`: two connections approve two pending CL requests of 1.5 each against a balance of 2. One succeeds; the other gives `leave_insufficient_balance`.
  - Isolation: HR of organisation B calling each function with A's ids gives `not_found` and nothing changes. Employees cannot call HR functions (`not allowed`). Public and maintenance modes cannot request.
